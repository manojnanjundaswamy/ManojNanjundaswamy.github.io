Media Studio Uploader

A private tool I use to publish my own videos to my own YouTube channels.

Media Studio Uploader is a small command-line program that runs only on my computer. When I finish a video, it uploads it to one of my YouTube channels with its title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and a scheduled publish time. It also shows me how my own videos perform (YouTube Analytics), the latest comments on them, and public information about topics and channels I research. It uses the YouTube API Services.

  • Who uses itOnly me (Manoj Nanjundaswamy), on channels I own. It is not offered, sold or available to anyone else.
  • What it accessesThrough Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0), on the channel I choose: uploading videos, thumbnails and captions, updating titles and descriptions, reading that channel's analytics and comments. It also reads public video and channel information for research.
  • What it storesOnly the sign-in tokens, kept privately on my own computer. Analytics and public data are shown on screen, not stored. No YouTube data is shared or sold.
