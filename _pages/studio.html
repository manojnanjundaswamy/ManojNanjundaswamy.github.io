Untangle Art

Closed test on Google Play

An arrow puzzle where every level hides a picture. Each arrow slides out the way it points, as long as nothing is in its path. Clear them in the right order and the board flips over to reveal what it was drawing all along.

  • PlayLevels from gentle to truly tangled, a daily puzzle, a gallery, hints and three hearts per level
  • FairNo ads in the first levels, never mid-puzzle, and videos only when you choose them
  • WhereAndroid phones and tablets, fully offline
  • BuiltReact and TypeScript in a Capacitor shell, with AdMob and Firebase

How a studio of one ships

Most of the studio is software I wrote for myself. The game is maybe half the work.

  • LevelsA level generatorTypeScriptTurns each picture into a solvable tangle of arrows, then checks it against quality rules (no dead ends, long enough arrows, a difficulty that rises steadily) before it ships.
  • MarketingTrailers and shorts in codeRemotionThe trailer and vertical videos are React compositions: a moving phone, a tapping finger and original music synced to each beat.
  • OperationsStudio HQElectronA private desktop app that finds every project on my machine and tracks its releases, store assets, uptime and to-dos, and runs routine jobs while I'm away. It works from my phone too.
  • ReleaseStore and websiteStatic siteScreenshots, feature graphics and the studio's app pages are generated from the same brand files, so everything matches.
