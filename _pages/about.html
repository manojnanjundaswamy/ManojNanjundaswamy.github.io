About

I studied mechatronics, robotics and automation, and came to software through the machines. It turned out I liked the part that told them what to do.

I started in 2019 in production support at Wolken Software, where I learned how real systems fail and how to keep them running. At BCITS I moved into building them: four Java web applications, APIs, security and the queries that slowed everything down. Since 2023 I've been a senior engineer at Amadeus, leading migrations and designing event-driven services for travel software.

I care about clean architecture, thorough tests and systems that don't page anyone at 2am.

Outside work I build with AI and make games. Vimora Studio is where those two meet: a one-person studio whose first game, Untangle Art, is in testing now.

I live in Bangalore and speak English, Kannada and Hindi. I'm happy to talk about senior backend and AI product roles, remote or in Bangalore.
